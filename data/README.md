# Senior AI Consultant Exercise

This folder contains the challenge brief and a fictional IT Service Desk data
package:

- `data/historical_tickets.csv`: 50 historical tickets. Category, priority,
  ownership, and resolution are historical examples, not guaranteed truth.
- `data/incoming_requests.jsonl`: 12 unlabeled requests for exploration,
  demonstration, or candidate-defined evaluation.
- `data/current_incidents.csv`: a small snapshot of active service incidents.
- `data/knowledge-base/`: 12 articles, including one explicitly deprecated
  article and guidance for shared-service incidents.

Treat the data as you would unfamiliar operational data: assess its quality,
decide what is relevant and trustworthy, and clearly state your assumptions.
You may use some or all files or add appropriately licensed or synthetic data.
Explain your choices and success criteria during the presentation.

All records, identities, systems, and company policies in this package are
fictional.
