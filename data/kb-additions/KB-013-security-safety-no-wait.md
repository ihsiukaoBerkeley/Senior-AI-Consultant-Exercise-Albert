# KB-013: Security and safety cases are routed without waiting

- Owner: Service Management
- Last reviewed: 2026-09-01
- Status: Active
- Applies to: Security incidents and physical safety hazards

Synthetic article written for this exercise; it is not part of the provided
data package.

Security and safety cases must not wait for missing details. Create the
ticket at once with the information available, give the user the immediate
steps from the relevant article, and flag the ticket for immediate staff
review. Ask for any missing details in a follow-up message.

Security cases include suspected phishing, unexpected MFA prompts, malware
alerts, lost or stolen devices and possible account compromise (KB-002,
KB-008). Safety cases include a swollen or overheating battery (KB-005).

Other requests may wait for the details their article requires.
